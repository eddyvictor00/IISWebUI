Transparency & Methodology - What this dashboard IS and IS NOT:
IS: This is a live-forward test on Alpaca PAPER trading (not real money) with 9 leveraged ETFs (CHAU,DRN,FAS,SPXL,TECL,WEBL,DGP,ERX,UCO), $6k per symbol (max $54k deployed, $46k cash buffer). The TR33 bot has been running continuously since - without manual trade edits. Chart starts at first live fill on 2026-04-30 - pre-inception -100% flatline removed. Trades are market orders at natural market prices. Every fill from Alpaca API with real filled_at and filled_avg_price.

IS NOT: This is NOT real-money P&L, NOT a backtest, NOT financial advice. Paper has $0 commission, zero slippage - real will be worse. We estimate $0.00 commission + $0.02/share slippage, but actual paper slippage not charged by Alpaca. Metrics include all trades, wins AND losses, no purging. Equity curve never reset. Return calculated on full $100k (industry standard), not $54k deployed. On deployed, return would be higher (~16.87%) but we report honest 9.11%. Past paper performance does not guarantee future real results.
